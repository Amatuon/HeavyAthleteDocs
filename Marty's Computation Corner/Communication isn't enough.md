# Too much of a good thing
By [Michael McDougal](https://heavyathlete.com/athlete/1/), aka [Marty the Scottish Mermaid](https://www.instagram.com/martythescottishmermaid/)
## Intro(vert)

I am an introvert. While there are many definitions of introverts and extroverts let me put forth the definition of I will be using during this article. 

> Introverts derive their sense of self worth from something intrinsic. Extroverts derive their sense of self worth from something extrinsic.

It's also a spectrum; I don't think anyone is 100% either and like most things in life most people fall somewhere in the middle. But that's the definition I'm using and if you don't agree I don't care because I'm about as introverted as you can be. I can count the number of people whose opinion of me matters to me on both hands and I don't even need all my fingers. Fun fact several of them are even dead. That's right I care more about the opinion of some dead people over yours and if that hurts your feelings I don't care. I genuinely don't want to hurt others but I also need to be upfront and tell you odds are I don't care about your perception of me. 


## The Modern Era

The return to monke people aren't quite right but there is a nugget of truth in their argument. The fact that I am an outlier in how introverted I am is a real problem. It wasn't that long ago that my stance would have been pretty normal. The access to information we have today is unprecedented in human history and our minds, bodies, and souls are trying to play catch up. I don't think all the information we have access to is bad. I make my living because of the information revolution. But things are allowed to have trade offs. Some things about the modern age are good, some things are bad, and some things are both. I don't know if their is a correct scientific term for what I am going to talk about but since I was in college I have internally called 'hyper-extroversion'. I noticed it spreading then but it has only become more rampant since. 


## The Internet and being Social

Every time our communication speeds up people communicate more. As more and more of our daily lives takes place while connected to the internet we are now communicating constantly. While this seems like this would be good for people there's a catch. Scarcity and price are inversely proportional. Replace price with value and you can see the problem clearly. Once upon a time communication could only occur face to face. At that point communication was valuable, if you wanted to send a message to someone a city away you either had to travel or send someone else to travel for you. As literacy began to rise we were able to communicate at range more easily. Sending a written message from town to town is significantly easier. Now a single person can carry 100's of messages at once and the written word is more permanent. We can still read the thoughts of some of the greatest minds in history. Literacy therefore also unlocked an asynchronous form of precise communication. Let's fast forward now to the present, the internet lets us communicate with basically everyone, living and dead, and to do it instantly! Before you think I'm getting mystical here when I say communicate with the dead I don't mean ghosts. I mean that I can watch lectures from the past online I can read books written before I was born and generally I can communicate in this asynchronous fashion with people from the past. That's a lot of people to talk to! Maybe even too many people! 

Now that communication is so abundant and easy its value has plummeted. We are so bombarded with communication that most interactions have no meaning either positive or negative because every interaction is easily replaceable. That's tragic. No wonder suicide rates are up, no wonder people feel more disconnected and alone, no wonder when we try to keep up with all of this we burn out. Because we are now so deficient in meaningful communication there's an unsatisfied need in so many people. I am truly fortunate that being an introvert I don't need very much validation from others. I know I can't properly relate to extroverts that aren't getting the level of validation they need, but I feel for you. In my opinion this is what has led to this 'hyper-extroversion'. In the modern era I don't think most extroverts can have this need filled and so they try to fill it with the tools of our age without realizing that the tools are causing the problem in the first place. 


## Wow that's a Bummer

I don't like just talking about problems, I think the technical term for that that is complaining. I like to discuss problems and then offer solutions. I won't say my solutions are perfect but I am offering them freely. I'm not going to stop using the internet. I'm not going to stop using social media. The return to monke people do go too far. But I am going to limit how I use them more. I have been bombarded in the last year with more communication on more channels than I can keep up with and it has impacted me negatively. So I'm not on call 24/7 anymore. I'm reprioritizing the people in my life that actually matter to me over everyone else. The hours I'm available for communication are Monday through Thursday (got to love the perks of being self employed). Also most of my channels for communication are now one directional. You can reply to the obviously automatically generated emails if you want to but I'm not reading my work email anymore (and I haven't read my personal in years). Social media is literally a form of nearly free advertising for my company so expect more [AI generated Walrus shit posts](https://www.instagram.com/martythescottishmermaid/) but don't message me I'm not going to reply by typing on my phone. If you want to talk to me about Heavy Athlete join the [discord](https://discord.com/invite/76C2WeTV9B). If you want to actually get my attention become a [premium member](https://heavyathlete.com/premium/signup/) on Heavy Athlete and join the private discord channel. 

I know not everyone is in a position to effectively shutdown multiple channels of communication but that's not how I view what I'm doing. I'm refocusing on who matters most. The Romans had a word "Pietas" which is the root word for piety. It means God, Country, Family, Self in the that order. As a libertarian I'm not sold on the order they used so here is mine: God, Family / Loved ones, Community, Self. I'm also applying one of the most valuable lessons I have ever learned, "You are more important than the victim. You can't help anyone if you are drowning too." The context for that was lifeguard training in high school but man does it ring true in so many places. My suggestion to everyone is simple. Find the communication in your life that's actually valuable and focus on that. And remember that lifeguard advice if you are drowning you need saving before you can save others. Take the time to get yourself right before you jump back in the deep end.
